## Locked demo decisions (partner outreach)

- **Use:** clickable static site **plus** a screenshot pack for cold email and a 20-minute walkthrough.
- **Practice story motion:** This House Would ban advertising targeted at children.
- **Matter tests / test leaderboard:** daily current events and news — **not** required to match the practice motion.
- **Format / role:** WSDC · **1st Proposition** (not Prime Minister).
- **Organisation in UI:** Debate Academy (generic; not Capstone).
- **Scope:** Full prompt pages (student + instructor) **plus** B2B marketing landing with Student demo / Instructor demo entry. No login/org-code screens.
- **Personas:** Logged-in student is **Sarah Chen**. She is also **#1** on the matter-file leaderboard (5/5). Instructor review is her correction.

## Locked product constraints

- Brand: **Arete**. Tagline: **Find Your Voice. Sharpen Your Argument.**
- Mission: Democratize elite debate training for every student.
- Dual-user: **students** + **instructors/organisations** (B2B beachhead, B2C later).
- Beachhead: Hong Kong debate academies/schools; launch partner **Capstone**.
- Core formats: **WSDC** now; **BP** expanding; other formats later.
- Differentiation: instructor dashboards, structured practice, **before-and-after correction workflow**, style matching — not a generic AI debate opponent.
- Current MVP: **non-functional demo of web page screenshots** to validate UX and secure partner commitments before building the full product.

## Visual system (pastel reinterpretation)

Proposal intent kept: trust blue, action terracotta, sage success, clay correction, lilac for premium. Executed as lower-saturation, warm-paper surfaces so the UI does not read as slate-Inter SaaS.

- Paper `#F3EEE6`, card `#FBF7F1`, ink `#2F2A24`, supporting copy `#5C534A`
- Dusty blue `#5A7A92`, terracotta `#C4846A`, sage `#6F8F78`, clay rose `#B5746E`, lilac `#8E7FA8`
- Wordmark and UI: Source Sans 3 (no display serif). Timers: IBM Plex Mono.
