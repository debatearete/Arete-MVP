# Overview — product functions

Source: `originals/Overview.pdf`

**Arete** is an AI-powered debate practice platform for debate academies and schools.

Students get targeted, actionable feedback on every speech (what to improve and how). Instructors get a window into student corrections so they can spend limited time on high-impact coaching.

- **Mission:** Democratize elite debate training for every student.
- **Tagline:** Find Your Voice. Sharpen Your Argument.
- **By Sept 2028:** 30 international partnerships, $100K profit, 10K+ students.

## Function 1: Student practice

### Selections

1. Public/private modes → organisation access
2. Choose debate format and prep time
3. Choose either motion / topic / difficulty / randomised
4. Choose speaker role

### Prep

1. **Motion bank**
   - Capstone docs
   - Debate data and other AI motion platforms
   - Tabbycat history
2. **Timer and lock screen** (similar to exam.net)
3. **Role**
   - Generate hypothetical speech bullet points
   - If selected speaker is a non-first speaker role → extra prep time for reading and writing rebuttals
   - Transcribed case files

### Speech and feedback

1. Give and record speech → video and voice recording (tech analogous to meeting/presentation analysis)
2. **Feedback**
   - Analytical strengths and gaps — **matter**
   - General + specific **style matching** (emulate credited speakers, HKT-specific) — **manner**
   - Feedback sheets + verbal feedback recordings
3. **Suggestion**
   - Instructs student to correct a particular segment of their speech
   - Instructor sees the before and after

## Function 2: Matter file tests with leaderboard

- Daily updates
- From news sources (compile database, Google News) → top results → simple 5-question quiz
- Instructor sees the scores
