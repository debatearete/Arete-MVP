Rebuttal drill

Identify and counter three arguments. For each, explain why it is (a) not true, (b) not relevant/important, and (c) even if the statement were true.

Motion: This House Would ban advertising targeted at children.

  1. Advertising only informs parents; children are not the real audience.
  2. Bans restrict commercial free speech and harm media that relies on ad revenue.
  3. Parents, not governments, should decide what children see.
