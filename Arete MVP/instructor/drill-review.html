Sarah Chen · Rebuttal · This House Would ban advertising targeted at children

Task

Identify and counter three arguments. For each: (a) not true, (b) not relevant/important, (c) even if true.

  1. Advertising only informs parents; children are not the real audience.
  2. Bans restrict commercial free speech and harm media that relies on ad revenue.
  3. Parents, not governments, should decide what children see.

Submission

1. While some ads are aimed at parents, child-directed branding is designed to create pester power — so the child is the true target even if the parent pays. 2. Commercial speech is already limited where harm is clear (tobacco). A targeted ban is narrower than “all advertising.” 3. Even if parents should decide, they cannot realistically police every screen; a ban changes the default environment.
